Clear-sketch effect (explosion.gif, explosion.wav):

The explosion footage and sound originate in Pyromania, a 1990s stock
explosion collection. Deltarune (Toby Fox) uses the same stock footage, but
neither Toby Fox nor Royal Sciences LLC owns it. These stock assets are not
covered by Solo's code license; if you hold rights to them and object to their
use here, contact @hunter.ahlquist.me on Bluesky and they will be removed.

The files were taken from the Kristal project's mirror.
